# Before you launch: things only you can decide

The policy pages (Privacy, Terms, Submission, Takedown, About, Contact) are **drafts** that describe what the software actually does. They are not legal advice.

- [ ] Fill in **Admin → Settings**: organisation name, public contact email, location. The amber "draft" banner on the policy pages disappears once a contact email is set.
- [ ] Have a lawyer review Privacy, Terms and Takedown for Tanzania and for any country your visitors are in.
- [ ] Decide which copyright notice process applies to you and edit `web/src/pages/StaticPages.tsx` accordingly.
- [ ] Only publish software you are allowed to redistribute. The dashboard forces you to tick a confirmation, but that is your legal responsibility, not a technical check.
- [ ] Remember that "Anyone with the link" Drive files can be copied and re-shared by visitors.
